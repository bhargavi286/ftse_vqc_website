How the prediction pipeline works

Three steps from raw indicators to a visual forecast.

①

Enter market indicators

Provide two numeric inputs — such as previous close price and trading volume — for the period you want to evaluate.

②

Run through the VQC model

Inputs are passed through a trained variational quantum classifier pipeline that scores the likely market direction.

③

Get a visual result

Instantly see the predicted value alongside a generated chart, so you can read the signal at a glance.
